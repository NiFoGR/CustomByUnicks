What to look for

The details that separate a good bathroom from a great one.

  • Lines that run through

    Joints that carry on from floor to wall, and niches that sit exactly on a tile line. That only happens when the room is set out before the first tile is fixed.

  • Light you see, fittings you do not

    The glow under a bench or along a niche, with no visible strip, dots or cables. The fittings are recessed into profiles and the drivers are kept accessible.

  • Stone that is cut, not trimmed

    Mitred corners and inlays cut to size rather than plastic edge trims. It takes longer and it is the difference you notice first.
