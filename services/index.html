One contract

Every trade, one point of contact.

A bathroom involves plumbing, electrics, waterproofing, plastering, tiling, joinery and glazing. When those are separate people, the problems live in the gaps: the tiler waiting on the plumber, the electrician who was never told where the mirror goes.

With UNICKS, one team plans the whole room and is responsible for all of it. You have one quote, one schedule and one number to call, and the person who designed your bathroom is accountable for how it is built.
