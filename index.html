Who we are

A bathroom is built once.
We build it properly.

Unick's is a UK bathroom specialist. We take a room back to brick and hand it back finished — first fix, tiling, brassware, lighting, glass and final seal.

Most bathrooms go wrong in the gaps between trades: the tiler waiting on the plumber, the electrician who was never told where the mirror light goes. We close those gaps by keeping the whole job in one pair of hands. The person who draws your bathroom is the person who builds it.

The result is what you see across our work — book-matched stone, lighting designed into the room rather than added to it, and joints that line through because they were set out on day one.

What we do

Four stages, one contract

You deal with us for all of it. One quote, one schedule, one number to call.

01

Design & visualisation

We measure up, work through layout options and produce a full 3D visual so you can walk the room before a single tile is ordered.

02

Supply & specification

Tiles, stone, sanitaryware, brassware, glass and lighting — specified, sourced and delivered to site on our accounts, at trade.

03

Strip-out & build

Removal, plumbing and electrical first fix, tanking, boarding, underfloor heating, tiling and second fix. Dust-sheeted, swept daily.

04

Finish & aftercare

Silicone, glass, decoration and a joint snag walk-through. Certificates issued, then we stay reachable long after the invoice clears.

Our signature

Dark or light

Two directions, one standard of build. Most clients know which one they are the moment they see them side by side.

Dark

Onyx and deep marble, terrazzo underfoot, matte black brassware and light hidden in the joints. A room that closes around you.

Light

Warm limestone and travertine, halo-lit mirrors, floating vanities and soft shadow gaps. A room that opens up.
