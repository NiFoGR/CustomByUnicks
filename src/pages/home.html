Why it lasts

The finish is what you see. The build is what lasts.

Most bathrooms that fail, fail behind the tiles: a shower waterproofed only around the tray, a floor that flexes, a light driver sealed into a wall. It rarely shows for a year or two, and then it shows on the ceiling below.

We build the parts you will never see to the same standard as the parts you will.

How we build {{arrow}}

  1. Waterproofed, not just sealed

    The whole wet zone is tanked before a tile goes on, so keeping water out never depends on grout or silicone.

  2. Floors that do not move

    Timber floors are stiffened and overboarded first. That is what stops stone and large tiles cracking later.

  3. Set out before it is fixed

    Every wall and floor is planned on paper, so cuts are balanced, lines run through and niches land on a joint.

  4. Everything stays reachable

    Valves, cistern mechanisms and LED drivers keep an access point, so a future repair never means breaking tiles.

Questions

Good to know

How much does a luxury bathroom cost?

It depends mostly on specification: stone, glass, brassware and lighting move the number far more than room size. Our packages start from the prices shown on the packages page, where you can see a guide price for your choices. After the survey you get a fixed, itemised quote, and that is the price you pay.

How long does a bathroom take?

Most full bathrooms take {{ph:2 to 4 weeks}} on site, depending on size and how much moves. You get a schedule with your quote, and we tell you straight away if anything changes.

Can we live at home while the work is done?

Usually, yes, as long as there is another toilet in the house. We protect the route in, keep the water off for the shortest time possible and clear up at the end of every day.

Do you supply everything, or do I buy the fittings?

Either works. Most clients let us supply everything, so it is specified to fit together and arrives when the build needs it. If you have already chosen pieces you love, we will design around them.
