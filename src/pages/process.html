How we design and build your bathroom

What happens, in what order, and what you get in writing. If you know exactly what to expect, there is nothing to worry about.

  1. 01

    Free design survey

    We visit, measure the room properly and look at what is behind it: where the soil pipe runs, which way the joists go, the water pressure, where power can come from. Then we talk about how you use the room, which matters more than any mood board.

    You get: an honest view of what is possible, and a rough budget range before you commit to anything.

  2. 02

    Design and specification

    We draw the layout, set out the tiles, place the drain and decide where every light goes. For larger rooms we produce a 3D visual so you can see the finished room before anything is ordered. Materials, brassware and glass are specified to work together.

    You get: drawings, a materials list, and a visual where the room needs one.

  3. 03

    Fixed, itemised quote

    Every fitting, every material and every stage of labour is listed and priced. If something at the survey could change the price, it is written down as a named allowance, not hidden in a total. Nothing is ordered until you approve the quote in writing.

    You get: one price, in writing, and dates booked in your diary.

  4. 04

    The build

    Strip-out, first fix plumbing and electrics, waterproofing, boarding, underfloor heating, tiling, then second fix and glass. One team runs the whole sequence, so nothing waits on a trade who has not turned up. The route through your home is protected and the site is cleared at the end of every day.

    You get: a schedule you can see, and one person to call.

  5. 05

    Handover and aftercare

    We walk the room with you and put right anything that is not exactly as it should be. You receive the certificates for notifiable electrical work, care notes for your stone and brassware, and manufacturer warranties registered in your name.

    You get: {{guarantee}}, and a number that still answers after the final invoice.

Behind the tiles

The standards you will never see.

These are the parts of a bathroom that decide whether it still looks right in ten years. They are also the parts most often rushed, because once the tiles are on, nobody can check them.

  • Waterproofing across the whole wet zone

    Showers and wet room floors are tanked with a complete waterproofing system, with sealed corners and pipe collars, before tiling starts. Grout and silicone are a finish, not a defence.

  • A floor that will not flex

    Timber floors are checked, stiffened where needed and overboarded so stone and large-format tiles have a rigid base. Movement is the main reason tiles crack and grout fails.

  • Falls and drainage set before tiling

    Shower floors are formed to a consistent fall so water reaches the drain and nowhere else. It is checked with water before a tile goes down.

  • Set out on paper first

    Every wall and floor is planned so cuts are balanced, joints line through and niches, valves and lights land exactly on the grid.

  • Access for the future

    Cistern mechanisms, valves and LED drivers keep a discreet access point, so maintenance never means breaking tiles.

  • Ventilation that actually clears the room

    Extraction sized for the room and ducted to the outside. Steam that lingers is what causes mould and failing silicone.
