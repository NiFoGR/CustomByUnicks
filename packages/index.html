Good to know

About the prices

Is the price on screen what I pay?

It is a guide for a typical bathroom of around 5m². After the free survey you get a fixed, itemised quote based on your actual room, and that is the price you pay. Nothing changes without your written approval.

My room is larger or an unusual shape.

Choose the package closest to what you want and send it anyway. We adjust for size, layout and anything structural at the survey, and explain every difference in writing.

Can I have something that is not listed?

Yes. Mention it in your message. Every room we build is designed for that house; the packages are simply the fastest way to show what things cost.

What is not included?

Structural work, moving soil stacks, replacing a boiler and redecorating outside the bathroom are quoted separately if you need them. If we find anything at the survey, you hear about it before you see a quote, not after.
